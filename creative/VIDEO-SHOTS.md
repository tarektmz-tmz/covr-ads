# Video formats that need real footage

These formats from the funnel plan can't be made from photos. Film them on a phone, vertical, 1080p, and upload to `media/`.

| Stage | Format | What to film (10–30 s) | Hook to open with |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU | Before & After | One racket after months unprotected (scuffs, edge wear) next to one kept in a COVR. Slow pan, same light. Use real rackets only. | "6 months of padel. Two very different rackets." |
| TOFU | BTS | Cutting canvas, stitching leather, a rejected prototype next to the final one, packing a box with dust bag. | "We rejected this prototype. Here's why." |
| TOFU | Founder-led | Founder to camera, holding a cover, 20 s. | "I didn't want to create another cheap nylon racket bag." |
| TOFU | Event | Covers on a courtside bench at a real tournament, players picking one up. | "We took COVR courtside for the first time." |
| MOFU | Comment response | Screenshot of a real comment, then founder answering. | "Someone asked why COVR costs more than a normal racket bag…" |
| MOFU | How-to | Racket in, zip closed, strap adjusted, ball pouch clipped to the D-ring. | "How to travel with your padel racket without damaging it." |
| BOFU | UGC | A real customer, unboxing to first match. | "I thought this was ridiculously expensive for a racket cover until…" |
| BOFU | Social proof | Real reviews/messages from customers (with their permission). | "Don't take our word for it." |

Not made yet because they need something real first:
- **Offer-first / Countdown / FOMO / Freebie**: need a real offer and end date (e.g. a free ball pouch), and the ball pouch is currently at 0 stock.
- **Social proof / Case study**: need real customer reviews or quotes.
- **Certification badges**: only if a material has a genuine certificate.
- **"Did you know?" heat claims**: verify the science before running.

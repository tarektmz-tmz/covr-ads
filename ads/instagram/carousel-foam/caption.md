## Caption
There's foam inside almost every padel racket. It's what gives you power and touch, and it doesn't like heat.

Leave a racket in direct sun or a hot car and the foam softens and wears out faster. Most rackets last 12 to 24 months if you play once or twice a week. How you look after yours decides which end you get.

COVR keeps it covered between games: out of the sun, off rough floors, and away from the keys and bottles in your bag.

One tip, cover or not: never leave your racket in a hot car.

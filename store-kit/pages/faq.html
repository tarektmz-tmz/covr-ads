What is the difference between Ceylon Cinnamon and regular cinnamon?

Most cinnamon sold in stores is Cassia, which contains high levels of coumarin. Ceylon Cinnamon, sometimes called true cinnamon, contains very low levels of coumarin and is widely considered the better choice for daily use. Ceylora uses only Ceylon Cinnamon.

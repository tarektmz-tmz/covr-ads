# COVR emails: abandoned cart and photo reviews (14 Oct 2026)

Voice: calm, short lines, no hype. Every fact below matches the shop's policies:
free international delivery (20–30 days from dispatch), free ball pouch with every cover,
made by hand in Bali, replace or refund if it arrives damaged.

---

## 1. Abandoned cart / checkout (Shopify Messaging, free)

Where: **Shopify admin → Marketing → Automations → Create automation**
- "Recover abandoned checkout" (people who reached checkout and left)
- "Recover abandoned cart" (people who added to cart and left, when Shopify knows their email)

Turn both on. Use the timings below. Replace the default text with this.

### Email 1 — 1 hour after
**Subject:** Your COVR is still here
**Preview:** Your cover and free ball pouch are saved in your cart.

> Your cover is waiting.
>
> We saved your cart, so it's ready when you are. Every COVR is made by hand in Bali,
> with a free ball pouch and free worldwide delivery.
>
> [Return to your cart]
>
> Questions about fit or delivery? Just reply to this email.

### Email 2 — 24 hours after
**Subject:** A quick answer before you decide
**Preview:** Fit, delivery and returns, in three lines.

> Still thinking it over? Here's what people usually ask.
>
> **Will it fit?** Most standard padel rackets. The cover is 39 × 29 cm with a 36 cm curved zip.
> **Delivery?** Free worldwide, 20 to 30 days from dispatch. DHL Express is available at checkout.
> **If something's wrong?** If it arrives damaged, we replace or refund it at our cost.
>
> [Complete your order]

### Email 3 — 72 hours after (only while COV20 is live, until 26 Oct)
**Subject:** 20% off your COVR, for a few more days
**Preview:** Use COV20 at checkout. Ends 26 October.

> Your cart is still saved.
>
> Use **COV20** at checkout for 20% off. Your free ball pouch stays free.
> The code ends on 26 October.
>
> [Use my 20% off]

After 26 October: delete Email 3, or swap in the $50 voucher once it exists.

## 2. Photo reviews from buyers (Judge.me)

Where: **Shopify admin → Apps → Judge.me → Requests**

1. **Review request email: ON.**
   Send it **35 days after fulfillment** (delivery takes 20–30 days from dispatch,
   so the cover has arrived and been used for a few days). If Judge.me shows
   "after delivery" for your carrier, use **7 days after delivery** instead.
2. **Reminder: ON**, 7 days after the first request, to people who haven't reviewed.
3. **Ask for photos:** in the request settings, turn on photo/video upload in the email form.
4. **Photo incentive (optional):** Judge.me → Settings → Review incentives (or "Coupons"),
   e.g. 10% off a next order or a free extra ball pouch for a review **with a photo**.
   It must be given for any honest review, good or bad, and the review should say it was
   incentivised (Judge.me adds this label automatically). Some incentive features need a paid
   Judge.me plan; check inside the app.

### Request email text
**Subject:** How's your COVR?
**Preview:** A photo of it on court would mean a lot.

> Your COVR has had a few weeks on court now. How is it?
>
> We're a small workshop in Bali and every review helps the next player decide.
> A photo of your cover (on court, in the car, on your shoulder) is the most helpful of all.
>
> [Leave a review]
>
> Thank you for choosing us.

### Reminder text
**Subject:** One minute, one photo?
> If you have a moment, we'd love to hear how your COVR is holding up, and see it in action.
> [Leave a review]

## 3. Asking customers who already bought

Where: **Judge.me → Requests → "Send requests to past orders"**
(called "Request reviews for previous orders" in some versions)

1. Choose all **fulfilled** orders older than ~25 days (so the cover has arrived).
2. Use the same request text as above.
3. Send once. Judge.me skips people who already reviewed.

Every review that comes in this way is from a real buyer and shows the **Verified buyer** badge.

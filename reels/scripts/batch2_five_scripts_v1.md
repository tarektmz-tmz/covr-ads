## B1. Before 2019 you could just walk in
Before twenty nineteen you could walk into a Rolex store and just buy one. No list. No history. You paid and you walked out with it. The steel sports models were always in demand, but you could still take one home that day. Then everything changed. Today the same watches have waiting lists that can take years, and some stores will not even take your name. Why? One word. Demand. I worked at Rolex for four years and I watched it happen from inside the store. Follow for more.

## B2. The price bubble
Rolex prices on the market went crazy, and then they dropped. Here is how it happened. First, people started buying watches just to sell them for a profit. Resellers pushed the prices higher and higher. Even regular people started buying any Rolex they could find, to flip it. The bubble kept growing until it burst. Prices came down, and a lot of watches went back on the market. Today the focus is back on the steel sports models. I am a former Rolex employee, and I watched the whole cycle from inside the store. DM me if you want to know what is happening with a specific model.

## B3. The waiting list is not first come, first served
Do you think the Rolex waiting list is first come, first served? It is not. Here is how it really works. Watches go to the clients with the strongest purchase history and the most loyalty, and they are given out by category. The dealer cannot order the exact watch you want from Switzerland. Rolex makes and packs the watches together, and the dealer gets whatever is ready. So being early on the list is not enough. Your history is what counts. I worked inside the system for four years. DM me and I will tell you where to start.

## B4. Build your profile without buying a watch you do not want
You want a steel Rolex, but the store says you need a purchase history. Here is the honest way. Start with a Datejust, the normal first model. You can also buy for your wife or your family through your own profile, like a ladies' Datejust or a Day-Date thirty-six. It builds your history without hurting your chance at a sports model. Be patient. Be polite. It is a long game. I am a former Rolex employee, and this is what I saw work. Follow for more.

## B5. How a new Rolex reaches the store
How does a brand new Rolex actually get to you? First, it is unveiled at Watches and Wonders in Switzerland, where only a few selected people try it. Then Rolex sends display pieces, without a movement, to selected stores, mostly the bigger dealers. After that, a wishlist opens, and the clients with the longest purchase history come first. Ask for one piece only. It can take up to about five months before the watch is officially on sale. And the market prices for new models often spike first, then drop. I saw this every year.

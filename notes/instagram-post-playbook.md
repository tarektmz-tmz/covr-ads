# Instagram post playbook (standing goal)
Every Instagram post for Advance Watch Co is built to get STOPS, SAVES, SENDS, comments and reposts. Decide the angle myself, do not ask.

- Cover: one bold promise + 3-4 big visuals, no clutter. Dark navy/charcoal, gold only as accent (no bright yellow glow). Curiosity line ("#1 is +134%").
- Carousel structure: hook, countdown/ranking (builds swipe-through), one chart per idea, a SAVE slide (cheat sheet / checklist), a SEND slide ("send to a friend who..."), then the course CTA last.
- Real numbers only, source on slide, UAE prices may differ, not-affiliated line on every post that shows Rolex.
- Caption: hook line, the list, then 3 calls to action (save / send / comment 1-5), course line, disclaimer, hashtags.
- Course: $99 (was $439), link in bio.

## Colour rule (from Tarek, Oct 30)
Use RED to flag anything under retail / cheap / a deal (banner 'UNDER RETAIL', savings tags). Our price box stays GREEN. Gold for brand accents, white for text.

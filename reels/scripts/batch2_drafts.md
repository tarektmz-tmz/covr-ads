## S4. The Rolex waiting list is NOT first come, first served (queue #2 and #24)
Think the Rolex waiting list is first come, first served? It's not. Here's how it really works. Watches go to the clients with the strongest purchase history and the most loyalty, in categories. The dealer can't order the exact watch you want from Switzerland. Rolex makes and packs the watches together, the dealer gets whatever is ready, and gives it out by the list. So being early on the list is not enough. Your history is what counts. I worked inside the system for four years. DM me and I'll tell you where to start.

Then Rolex sends display pieces, without a movement, to selected stores, mostly the bigger dealers. After that, a wishlist opens, and the clients with the longest purchase history are first. Ask for one piece only. It can take up to about five months before the watch is officially on sale, and the market prices for new models often spike first, then drop. I saw this every year.

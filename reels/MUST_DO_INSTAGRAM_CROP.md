# MUST DO, EVERY TIME (owner rule, Nov 19): INSTAGRAM CROPS THE EDGES. NEVER FORGET.

Every reel, story, thumbnail, cover, pop-up, caption bar and corner window MUST be designed for Instagram's crop BEFORE it is rendered, and checked with an overlay BEFORE it is sent. The owner must never have to repeat this.

## Reel and story frame (1080 x 1920)
- Safe zone: keep ALL text, faces, logos, captions, windows and pop-ups inside about 54 px at the sides, 115 px at the top and 100 px at the bottom. Scale content to about 88 percent.
- The bottom caption bar, the avatar corner window and call-to-action pop-ups must sit inside that zone.

## Thumbnails and covers (profile grid is cropped to 3:4)
- The grid shows only the CENTRE 1080 x 1440 (from y = 240 to y = 1680). Anything outside that band is cut off in the grid.
- Put the hook, the big number, the face/watch and the banner inside BOTH that band AND the safe zone above (sides at least 54 px).
- Check with an overlay (green = 3:4 grid crop, red = safe zone) before sending. Do not send a thumbnail that fails the overlay.

## Check procedure (do it, do not skip)
1. Render a frame (or the thumbnail) and overlay: red rectangle (54,115)-(1026,1820); for thumbnails also green (0,240)-(1080,1680).
2. Look at it. Anything touching or crossing a line = fix it first.
3. Only then send it to the owner. Mention in one line that the crop check was done.

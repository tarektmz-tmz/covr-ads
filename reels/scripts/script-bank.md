## 01 — 3 checks before you buy any Rolex
Hook: "Before you pay for any Rolex in Dubai, check these three things."
Script: Before you pay for any Rolex in Dubai, check these three things. First, ask for photos or a video of the exact watch, with today's date, showing the reference and the serial number. Second, make sure it is a full set, and that the numbers on the watch match the card and the papers. Third, look at the warranty date. A brand new watch should have a recent one. If a seller does not want to show you any of this, walk away.

## 02 — The seconds hand myth
Hook: "A sweeping seconds hand does not prove a Rolex is real."
Script: A lot of people think that if the seconds hand glides smoothly, the Rolex is real. That is only half the story. A ticking hand tells you the watch is fake, but a smooth sweep proves nothing, because the better fakes sweep too. So also check the Cyclops, which should magnify the date two and a half times, the tiny crown etched on the crystal at six o'clock, and the weight of the watch and the bracelet.
Scenes: WATCH (Submariner) / CARD Cyclops 2.5x / CARD crown at 6 / END

## 03 — Papers don't prove it's real
Hook: "Box and papers do not make a watch genuine."
Script: Box and papers do not make a watch genuine. Boxes and cards are faked, and real papers are sometimes swapped onto a different watch. So always check the watch itself. The reference and the serial must match the card and the papers, and the watch must look right in every detail. Papers help, but they are never the proof on their own. And keep everything, the card, the papers and the messages, for servicing or if you ever resell.
Scenes: CARD "Papers ≠ proof" / CARD numbers must match / CARD keep everything / END

## Research tools

**Free**
- Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library): active ads, start date, number of ad versions. Tip: set country to France or Germany to see reach numbers.
- TikTok Creative Center (ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter): top ads with likes and CTR, trending products
- Koala Inspector (Chrome extension): a store's best sellers, theme, apps
- Similarweb (free version): estimated monthly visits
- who.is / Wayback Machine: store age

**Paid**
- PiPiADS: TikTok ads (views, spend)
- AdSpy: Facebook/Instagram ads, sort by likes and comments (~$149/mo)
- PPSPY: store sales tracking (has a free plan with limits)
- Shophunter, Winning Hunter: store revenue estimates
- StoreLeads: search Shopify stores by keyword, sales and traffic filters
- Minea: already tried, did not like it
